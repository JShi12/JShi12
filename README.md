# Jing Shi

Data scientist and machine learning engineer with a PhD and 7+ years of research experience in
materials and physical systems. I build computer vision and quantitative image analysis for
scientific imagery, agentic AI and LLM systems for scientific work, and Bayesian methods
(Gaussian processes, Bayesian optimisation, active learning) for physical and engineering data,
all with tested, reproducible code.

## Featured projects

- **[SEM wafer-defect analysis](https://github.com/JShi12/sem-wafer-defects)**:
  segmentation, measurement, classification and registration of semiconductor SEM images,
  comparing classical and deep-learning methods against expert ground truth. U-Net Dice
  0.960–0.981 against expert masks, Bland–Altman-validated defect sizes, dataset
  shortcut audits, and an image-registration benchmark (ECC 99.3% within 1 px); shipped as a
  tested command-line tool.
- **[AI Literature Review Assistant](https://github.com/JShi12/AI-literature-review-assistant)**:
  an agentic LLM system (PydanticAI) that turns research PDFs into citation-grounded
  literature reviews, with sentence-level traceability and an evaluation harness of calibrated LLM judges and a CI regression gate. Live demo.
- **[Boiling-point prediction: pretrained language model study, active learning & Bayesian optimisation](https://github.com/JShi12/Predict-Organic-Chemical-Boiling-Point)**:
  nested cross-validation and a label audit; a pre-registered test of a pretrained chemical
  language model (MoLFormer-XL), which matched curated descriptors on familiar chemistry but
  generalised worse to unseen chemistry, with conformal prediction intervals checked under
  distribution shift; and BoTorch Bayesian optimisation that finds 69% of in-spec compounds
  after 12% of experiments.
- **[Vehicle damage detection](https://github.com/JShi12/vehicle-damage-detection)**:
  YOLO11 with MLflow tracking, a containerised FastAPI service, and CI with
  champion/challenger model promotion.
